Hello World!

When I was a kid at school, we had a funny way to find out how much a boy and a girl love each other: we wrote it down by hand, just like this table. That gave me the idea to make it digital.

How it works: the first row shows how many times each letter of "I LOVE YOU" appears in the two names. In every next row, two neighbouring numbers are added together and only the last digit is kept (for example 7 + 5 = 12 → 2). The numbers left at the bottom are your love percentage!
